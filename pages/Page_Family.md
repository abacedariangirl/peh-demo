## Josephine Pinder
Josephine Emily Pinder was born in autumn 1877 in Victoria BC, the first child of William George Pinder and Anne Marie Devereux. William had come to BC at the request of his uncle Lieutenant-Governor Joseph Trutch. Josephine was named after her great-uncle Joseph, and her grandmother Emily.
